Our Story

At P & P Pet Shop, our journey began with a simple love for animals and a desire to serve the growing community of pet lovers in Mount Lavinia. From humble beginnings, we've grown into a trusted name, offering carefully selected food, accessories, and care products for dogs, cats, birds, hamster, and more.

Our store is more than just a pet supply shop — it's a place where pet parents find expert advice, friendly service, and a passion for happy, healthy animals. Every product on our shelves is chosen with care, and every customer is treated like family.

Whether you're a first-time pet owner or a seasoned animal lover, we're here to support you every step of the way - because at P & P Pet Shop, your pet's well-being is our top priority.

Who We Are

P & P Pet Shop is a locally owned and operated pet supply store located in the heart of Mount Lavinia. We specialize in providing high-quality products and trusted care essentials for all kinds of pets — from playful pups and curious kittens to chirping birds and cute hamsters.

Backed by a team of passionate animal lovers, we're here to help every pet owner find exactly what they need to keep their companions happy, healthy, and loved.

At P & P, it's not just about products — it's about building lasting relationships with our customers and their pets.
